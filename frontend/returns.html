Returns Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. Our 30-day return policy makes it easy.

30-Day Easy Returns

Return items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund or exchange

Return Conditions

30-Day Window

Items must be returned within 30 days of the original delivery date. This gives you plenty of time to test and evaluate your equipment.

Original Condition

Products must be in original, unused condition with all original packaging, accessories, manuals, and documentation included.

Proof of Purchase

Original receipt or order confirmation is required. This helps us process your return quickly and accurately.

No Damage

Items must not show signs of misuse, damage, or normal wear. Professional equipment should be handled with care.

How to Return an Item

1

Contact Us

Call us at +237 6 80 49 49 49 or email info@soundlightpro.com to initiate your return. We'll provide you with a Return Authorization (RA) number.

2

Package Your Item

Carefully package the item in its original box with all accessories. Include the RA number and a copy of your receipt inside the package.

3

Ship the Return

Ship the package to our returns center using the prepaid shipping label we provide (for eligible returns) or arrange your own shipping.

4

Processing & Refund

Once we receive your return, we'll inspect the item and process your refund within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

Return Categories

Standard Returns

  • Reason: Changed mind, not what expected, found better price elsewhere
  • Return Window: 30 days from delivery
  • Condition: Must be unopened/unused with original packaging
  • Return Shipping: Customer responsibility
  • Refund: Full purchase price (minus original shipping if applicable)
  • Processing Time: 5-7 business days after receipt

Defective Items

  • Reason: Manufacturing defect, doesn't work as intended
  • Return Window: 30 days from delivery (warranty may extend this)
  • Condition: As received, no additional damage
  • Return Shipping: Free prepaid label provided
  • Refund: Full refund including original shipping
  • Processing Time: 3-5 business days after receipt

Wrong Item Sent

  • Reason: We sent the wrong product by mistake
  • Return Window: 30 days from delivery
  • Condition: Any condition (our error)
  • Return Shipping: Free prepaid label provided
  • Refund: Full refund including shipping, or free exchange
  • Processing Time: 1-3 business days after receipt

Damaged in Transit

  • Reason: Package damaged during shipping
  • Return Window: Must be reported within 3 days of delivery
  • Condition: Document damage with photos
  • Return Shipping: Free prepaid label provided
  • Refund: Full refund including shipping, or replacement
  • Processing Time: 1-3 business days after photos received

Exchanges

Product Exchanges

Want a different model or color? We offer free exchanges within 30 days. The new item must be of equal or greater value. If there's a price difference, you'll be charged or refunded accordingly.

Upgrades

Upgrade to a better model within 30 days and pay only the difference. This is perfect if you realize you need more features or power than your original purchase.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items cannot be returned unless defective:

  • Custom or special-order items
  • Software or digital downloads (once activated)
  • Items damaged by misuse or abuse
  • Items returned without Return Authorization number
  • Items returned after 30 days
  • Consumable items (batteries, cables if opened)

Refund Information

Payment Method

Refunds are processed to your original payment method. Credit card refunds may take 3-5 business days to appear on your statement. Bank transfer refunds are processed within 1-2 business days.

Refund Amount

You'll receive a full refund of the purchase price. Original shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to our error or a defective product. Return shipping costs are deducted unless we provide a prepaid label.
