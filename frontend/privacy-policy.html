Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

SoundLightPro is committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

Quick Summary

  • We collect information to process orders and improve our services
  • We never sell your personal information to third parties
  • We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data
  • You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data
  • We use cookies to enhance your browsing experience

Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you create an account, place an order, or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name and contact information (email, phone, address)
  • Billing and shipping addresses
  • Payment information (processed securely by third-party providers)
  • Account credentials and preferences
  • Communication history with our support team

Usage Information

We automatically collect information about how you use our website:

  • Pages visited and time spent on our site
  • Products viewed and search queries
  • Device information (browser, operating system, IP address)
  • Referral sources and exit pages
  • Shopping cart and purchase history

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • Remember your preferences and login status
  • Analyze website traffic and user behavior
  • Personalize content and product recommendations
  • Improve our website performance and security
  • Enable social media features and targeted advertising

How We Use Your Information

Order Processing

Process your orders, handle payments, arrange shipping, and provide customer support.

Communication

Send order confirmations, shipping updates, support responses, and important account information.

Service Improvement

Analyze usage patterns to improve our website, products, and customer experience.

Marketing

Send promotional emails and product recommendations (with your consent and opt-out options).

Security

Protect against fraud, unauthorized access, and maintain the security of our systems.

Legal Compliance

Comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our terms of service.

How We Share Information

Service Providers

We share information with trusted third-party service providers who help us operate our business:

  • Payment Processors: Stripe, PayPal (for secure payment processing)
  • Shipping Companies: DHL, local courier services (for order delivery)
  • Email Services: For sending transactional and marketing emails
  • Analytics: Google Analytics (for website performance analysis)
  • Customer Support: Help desk and chat services

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law or to:

  • Comply with legal processes, court orders, or government requests
  • Protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of others
  • Investigate and prevent fraud or other illegal activities
  • Enforce our terms of service or other agreements

Business Transfers

In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred to the new entity, subject to the same privacy protections.

Data Security

Encryption

All sensitive data is encrypted in transit using SSL/TLS and at rest using industry-standard encryption.

Secure Infrastructure

Our servers are hosted in secure data centers with physical and digital access controls.

Access Controls

Access to personal data is limited to authorized personnel who need it to perform their job functions.

Regular Monitoring

We continuously monitor our systems for security threats and vulnerabilities.

Note: While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong passwords and keep your account information confidential.

Your Privacy Rights

Access

Request a copy of the personal information we have about you.

Correction

Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

Deletion

Request deletion of your personal information (subject to legal requirements).

Portability

Request a machine-readable copy of your personal information.

Opt-out

Unsubscribe from marketing emails or opt-out of certain data processing.

Object

Object to certain types of data processing for legitimate interests.

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@soundlightpro.com or call +237 6 80 49 49 49.

Cookie Policy

Essential Cookies

Required for basic website functionality, including shopping cart, security, and user authentication. These cannot be disabled.

Performance Cookies

Help us understand how visitors use our website by collecting anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics.

Functional Cookies

Remember your preferences, language settings, and personalization choices to enhance your experience.

Marketing Cookies

Used to deliver relevant advertisements and track the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns.

You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings or our cookie consent banner. Note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Data Retention

  • Account Information: Retained while your account is active and for 3 years after account closure
  • Order History: Kept for 7 years for accounting and warranty purposes
  • Marketing Data: Retained until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Website Analytics: Anonymized data retained for 26 months
  • Support Communications: Kept for 3 years for quality assurance

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than Cameroon, including the United States and European Union, where our service providers are located. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place to protect your information in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we have collected information from a child under 16, please contact us immediately.

Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes by email or through a prominent notice on our website. Your continued use of our services after such changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
