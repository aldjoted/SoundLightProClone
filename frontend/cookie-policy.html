Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how SoundLightPro uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website to enhance your browsing experience.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile) when you visit a website. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences over time, so you don't have to keep re-entering them whenever you come back to the site or browse from one page to another.

Benefits of Cookies:

  • Remember your login status and preferences
  • Keep items in your shopping cart between sessions
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations
  • Analyze website performance and user behavior
  • Enable social media features and integrations
  • Improve website security and prevent fraud

Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

Required

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility.

Examples:
  • Session ID: Maintains your session while browsing
  • Authentication: Keeps you logged in
  • Shopping Cart: Remembers items in your cart
  • Security: Prevents cross-site request forgery
  • Load Balancing: Distributes website traffic

Duration: Session or up to 1 year

Can be disabled: No (website won't function properly)

Performance Cookies

Optional

These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, helping us improve performance and user experience.

Examples:
  • Google Analytics: Website usage statistics
  • Page Load Times: Performance monitoring
  • Error Tracking: Identifies technical issues
  • Traffic Sources: How users find our site
  • Popular Pages: Most visited content

Duration: 26 months

Can be disabled: Yes

Functional Cookies

Optional

These cookies allow the website to remember choices you make and provide enhanced, personalized features.

Examples:
  • Language Preference: Your chosen language
  • Currency Settings: Preferred currency display
  • Recently Viewed: Products you've looked at
  • Chat Settings: Chatbot preferences
  • Theme Choice: Light/dark mode preferences

Duration: 1 year

Can be disabled: Yes (some features may not work)

Marketing Cookies

Optional

These cookies track visitors across websites to display relevant advertisements and measure marketing campaign effectiveness.

Examples:
  • Facebook Pixel: Social media advertising
  • Google Ads: Search and display advertising
  • Retargeting: Show ads for viewed products
  • Conversion Tracking: Measure ad effectiveness
  • Email Marketing: Personalized email campaigns

Duration: 90 days to 2 years

Can be disabled: Yes

Managing Your Cookie Preferences

Cookie Consent Banner

When you first visit our website, you'll see a cookie consent banner allowing you to:

  • Accept all cookies
  • Reject non-essential cookies
  • Customize your preferences by category
  • View detailed information about each cookie type

Browser Settings

You can also control cookies through your browser settings:

  • Block all cookies from all websites
  • Block third-party cookies only
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Set up notifications when cookies are sent
Browser Help Pages:
Chrome Firefox Edge Safari

Opt-Out Tools

Use these industry tools to opt out of targeted advertising:

  • Digital Advertising Alliance
  • Your Online Choices (EU)
  • Google Ad Settings
  • Facebook Ad Preferences

Impact of Disabling Cookies

Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect your experience on our website:

  • Essential cookies: Website may not function properly
  • Performance cookies: We can't improve the site based on usage data
  • Functional cookies: Personalization features won't work
  • Marketing cookies: You may see less relevant advertisements

Mobile Applications

Our mobile applications may use similar tracking technologies. You can manage these through your device settings:

Android

  • Go to Settings > Privacy > Ads
  • Enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"
  • Reset your advertising ID if needed

iOS

  • Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Apple Advertising
  • Turn off "Personalized Ads"
  • Use "Ask App Not to Track" when prompted
