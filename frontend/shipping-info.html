Important Shipping Information

Processing Time

Orders placed before 2:00 PM (GMT+1) Monday-Friday are processed the same day. Weekend orders are processed on the next business day.

Tracking

All shipments include tracking. You'll receive a tracking number via email once your order ships. Track your package on our website or the carrier's site.

Insurance

All shipments are insured against loss or damage during transit. High-value items may require additional signature confirmation.

Documentation

For international orders, we handle all export documentation and customs declarations. Additional duties may apply at destination.

Restrictions

Some items may have shipping restrictions. Lithium batteries and certain chemicals have special requirements. Contact us for details.

Holidays

Shipping and processing are suspended on public holidays. Orders placed during holidays will be processed on the next business day.
