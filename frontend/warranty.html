Warranty Information

We stand behind our products with comprehensive warranty coverage and expert support.

2-Year Comprehensive Warranty

All SoundLightPro products come with a 2-year manufacturer warranty covering parts and labor

What's Covered

Manufacturing Defects

Any defects in materials or workmanship that occur under normal use conditions are fully covered.

Parts & Labor

Both replacement parts and labor costs are covered for warranty repairs at authorized service centers.

Free Shipping

We cover shipping costs both ways for warranty repairs within Cameroon during the warranty period.

Replacement Units

If repair isn't possible, we'll provide a replacement unit of the same or equivalent model.

Technical Support

Free technical support and troubleshooting assistance throughout the warranty period.

Quick Turnaround

Most warranty repairs are completed within 7-10 business days of receiving your unit.

Warranty Terms by Product Category

Audio Equipment

  • Coverage: 2 years from purchase date
  • Includes: Speakers, amplifiers, mixers, microphones
  • Covers: Electronic components, drivers, power supplies
  • Service: Authorized repair centers

Lighting Equipment

  • Coverage: 2 years from purchase date
  • Includes: LED fixtures, controllers, moving lights
  • Covers: Electronics, motors, LED arrays
  • Note: LED life expectancy varies by usage

Accessories

  • Coverage: 1 year from purchase date
  • Includes: Cables, stands, cases, adapters
  • Covers: Manufacturing defects only
  • Note: Normal wear and tear not covered

Digital Equipment

  • Coverage: 2 years from purchase date
  • Includes: Digital mixers, interfaces, processors
  • Covers: Hardware defects, firmware support
  • Updates: Free firmware updates included

How to File a Warranty Claim

1

Contact Support

Call +237 6 80 49 49 49 or email warranty@soundlightpro.com with your order number and description of the issue.

2

Troubleshooting

Our technical team will guide you through troubleshooting steps to determine if the issue can be resolved without repair.

3

Authorization

If repair is needed, we'll issue a Return Authorization (RA) number and provide shipping instructions for sending your unit.

4

Repair & Return

We'll repair your unit and return it to you, typically within 7-10 business days of receiving it at our service center.

What's Not Covered

Physical Damage

Damage from drops, impacts, water, fire, or other physical trauma is not covered under warranty.

Misuse or Abuse

Damage resulting from improper use, modifications, or operation outside specified parameters.

Normal Wear

Cosmetic wear, fader wear, button wear, and other normal wear from regular use.

Power Issues

Damage from power surges, incorrect voltage, or electrical problems in your facility.

Unauthorized Repair

Units serviced by non-authorized technicians void the warranty coverage.

Expired Warranty

Claims filed after the warranty period has expired cannot be honored under warranty terms.
