Frequently Asked Questions

Orders & Payment

How do I place an order?

You can place an order by browsing our products, adding items to your cart, and proceeding to checkout. You'll need to provide shipping information and payment details to complete your purchase.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept major credit cards (Visa, MasterCard, American Express), PayPal, bank transfers, and mobile money payments. All transactions are secure and encrypted.

Can I modify or cancel my order?

Orders can be modified or cancelled within 2 hours of placement, provided they haven't been processed yet. Please contact our support team immediately for assistance.

Shipping & Delivery

How long does shipping take?

Delivery times vary by location. Within Douala: 1-2 business days. Other Cameroon cities: 3-5 business days. International shipping: 7-14 business days depending on destination.

Do you offer free shipping?

Yes! We offer free shipping on orders over $500 within Cameroon. For smaller orders or international shipments, standard shipping rates apply.

How can I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can use this number on our website or the carrier's website to track your package.

Products & Technical

Do you provide technical specifications?

Yes, detailed technical specifications are available on each product page. If you need additional information, our technical team is available to help.

Do you offer installation services?

We offer professional installation services for permanent setups. Contact our team to discuss your requirements and get a quote for installation services.

What if I receive a defective product?

If you receive a defective product, contact us immediately. We'll arrange a replacement or repair under warranty. Please keep the original packaging for returns.
